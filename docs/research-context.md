# Research Context

## Problem

Project portfolio management is often strong at tracking delivery but weaker at continuously connecting projects to **measurable value realization**. Benefits may be described inconsistently, measured late, or treated as isolated project outcomes rather than portfolio-level signals.

This project explores how analytics and AI can support a value-management process in which benefits are identified, planned, monitored, transferred, and sustained over time.

## Value perspective

The prototype treats value as multidimensional and contextual. Depending on the portfolio, value may include financial outcomes, stakeholder satisfaction, capability building, talent development, knowledge creation, strategic alignment, collaboration, and other measurable benefits.

## Research question behind the prototype

**How can AI help project and portfolio leaders make benefits more explicit, measurable, and actionable from project formulation through benefit realization?**

## Current contribution

The current PoC operationalizes two early decision-support capabilities:

- discovering benefits implicit in existing project deliverables; and
- recommending changes to proposed objectives and deliverables to strengthen benefit realization.

The broader research direction extends this toward predictive and prescriptive portfolio value management.
